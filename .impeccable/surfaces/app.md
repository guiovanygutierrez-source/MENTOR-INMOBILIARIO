# Application surface brief

Mode: Operate

## Visitor's task

Learn how to evaluate a home purchase or property investment, practice on realistic situations, and ask Mentor when a concept or decision is unclear.

## First viewport

The dashboard leads with one recommended next step tied to the learner's saved route. A crisp plan diagram offers direct paths into budgeting, legal checks, and acquisition steps. The primary action resumes the lesson; a secondary action opens Mentor for a question.

## Signature interaction

Select a labeled zone of an illustrative home plan to reveal the associated module or practice. The plan is educational geometry, not a real property listing.

## States and constraints

Preserve user progress, unlock rules, auth and account persistence, AI opt-in/privacy copy, local mentor fallback, Gemini user key flow, videos, search sources, simulators, glossary, and saved exercises. Include empty, completed, locked, error, and loading states without inventing product facts. Maintain keyboard access, reduced motion, and touch-friendly layouts.
